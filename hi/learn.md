# Learn

## Intent

Not everyone arrives holding something. Some people are deciding whether to, and the server is where they decide.

So the parts of this that simply explain are not an afterthought around the holder features: what the token is, where to trade it, how the community is doing. They are the front door, and they should work for someone who holds nothing and has linked no wallet.

The other half is trust in the answer. A bot that is half-awake gives confident wrong answers, so a member should be able to find out cheaply whether it is really there before believing what it just told them.

Both halves change shape once anybody can run this for their own community. Everything the explaining surfaces say is now somebody's configuration: what the token is called, how precise it is, where it can be traded, which parts are switched on, what a hand pays. A page that answers from what the software shipped with, rather than from what this server is running, is worse than no page at all. It is confidently wrong, and the member reading it is the one person with no way to tell.

## Criteria

- **LEARN-1**  I can find out what the token is and where to trade it without leaving Discord
- **LEARN-2**  I can see how the community is doing as a whole, not only myself
- **LEARN-3**  I can tell whether the bot is awake before I trust an answer from it
- **LEARN-4**  I can find out what the bot does from inside Discord, without someone telling me
- **LEARN-5**  I can read a game's rules before I decide to play, not only once I am in a hand
- **LEARN-6**  The rules I am shown are the rules the bot actually plays by
- **LEARN-7**  What I am told about the token is what the people running this server chose to tell me
  - **LEARN-7.a**  Every place I am sent to trade it or look it up is one somebody here picked, and if nobody picked any I am told that rather than sent somewhere at random
  - **LEARN-7.b**  An amount I read is in this server's own token at its own precision, so it matches what my wallet shows me
- **LEARN-8**  What I am shown the bot can do is what it can do in this server
  - **LEARN-8.a**  I do not read about a part of it that is switched off here and then go looking for something that is not there
  - **LEARN-8.b**  The numbers in a game's rules are the ones this server plays by, not the ones it was shipped with
