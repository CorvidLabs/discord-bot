# Show

## Intent

People do not collect these to look at them alone. The collection is the social object, so showing it off has to be one action and it has to look good: a card with the actual picture, in the channel, without a link anyone has to click.

Two things this must never do: put someone's wallet address in front of the room as the price of showing a picture, and show a blank frame. Where a picture comes from, and what keeps the last good one when a fetch fails, is PICTURE. What this family owes is that a card with nothing to show says so out loud, because that is the bot's problem to name rather than the holder's to wonder about.

Standing is the same instinct pointed inward. A member wants to know where they sit among the other holders, and they want that answer to be about this server: its own token, its own tiers, its own collections, however many of each there happen to be. A server with one collection should not be shown a gap where a second one used to be, and a server with ten should not have eight of them quietly dropped on the way to the card.

## Criteria

- **SHOW-1**  I can put one of my NFTs in the channel so people can see it
  - **SHOW-1.a**  If its picture will not load, I am told that rather than shown an empty frame
  - **SHOW-1.b**  Showing it off does not put my wallet address in front of everyone
- **SHOW-2**  I can see how I stand against the other holders
  - **SHOW-2.a**  I can keep my name off that list if I would rather not be seen
- **SHOW-3**  I can look through everything I own without it filling the channel
- **SHOW-4**  What I can show off, and where I am told I stand, are about this server's own collections, however many it has
  - **SHOW-4.a**  I can show off a piece from any collection this server has, not from a fixed pair somebody else picked
  - **SHOW-4.b**  In a server with no collections at all, my standing is still a page about what I hold rather than a page of empty headings
