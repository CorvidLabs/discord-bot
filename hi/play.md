# Play

## Intent

This is the part with no business reason. It exists so that the server is somewhere people hang around rather than somewhere they check.

Because of that, the one thing that would ruin it is any suggestion that the chips are worth money. The moment somebody believes they can cash out, a card table with real stakes has been built by accident, and every fun decision becomes a financial one. Chips are a score. There is no path from a game to the wallet that pays people, in either direction, there is no conversion, and no hand ever changes what anybody is owed.

Play is private, and that is not only taste: it is the only thing stopping somebody else touching your table. An earlier version had a hand the whole channel voted on, and it went, because a table the room can play is not your table. What the room gets is the result: a natural, a big win, a run of calls. Seen, not touchable.

Two more things follow from this being something other people run. Whoever runs it may not want a card table in their community at all, so the games have to be a thing they can leave switched off rather than a thing they have to explain. And playing has to be free for them as well as for the member: a game reads only what the bot already knows and never asks the chain a question, because the person paying for those questions is the operator, and a busy evening of blackjack must not be the reason roles stop syncing in the morning.

## Criteria

- **PLAY-1**  There is something to do in the server that costs me nothing real
  - **PLAY-1.a**  What I win cannot be turned into money, and nobody is left thinking it can
- **PLAY-2**  My hand is mine: nobody else can play it or look at it
- **PLAY-5**  The games stay in their own corner instead of filling up the main channel
- **PLAY-6**  A hand good enough to be worth telling people about is told to them, and I do not have to do the telling
- **PLAY-7**  Nothing the room sees is something the room can touch
- **PLAY-8**  Running out of chips is a pause, not the end: something free is always there and the game tells me where
- **PLAY-9**  I can run this without the games, so a community that does not want a card table does not get one
- **PLAY-10**  The words the games use are mine: what the chips are called, what the dealer is called, what each game is called
- **PLAY-11**  However much anybody plays, it never costs me a request to the chain or a unit of the token
